# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge (summary)

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness,
respect for differing opinions, gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback, and
focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised
language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without
their explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at imadbouftini04@gmail.com. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be
respected. The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at the link above.
